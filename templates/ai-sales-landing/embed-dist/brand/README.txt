BRAND ASSETS
============

Replace these placeholder files with your own, keeping the same filenames
(or update the paths in src/brand.config.ts → logo / favicon / ogImage).

  logo.svg      Your wordmark/logo. Shown in the nav + footer.
                Recommended: SVG, or a transparent PNG ~ 160x32.
  favicon.svg   Browser tab icon. SVG or 32x32 / 48x48 PNG.
  og.svg        Social share preview image.
                IMPORTANT: most social platforms (Facebook, LinkedIn, X) do
                NOT render SVG share images. Before launch, export a 1200x630
                PNG or JPG (e.g. og.png) and point brand.config.ts → ogImage at it.

Everything else (colours, copy, links) lives in src/brand.config.ts.
